How to read Peppy

Laugh first.
Look closer.

At Curious depth, learn the central idea. At College depth, name the components. At Mechanism depth, test where the analogy stops and what evidence a professional would need.

The images are AI-generated with a consistent Peppy reference. Dialogue, citations, labels and corrections are editable text outside the art. No image on this site is a molecular-structure diagram.

A boundary worth keeping

Peppy is not your prescriber.

This is science education and satire, not medical advice, a drug recommendation or a place to buy peptides. Endogenous hormones and manufactured medicines are different subjects. Specific clinical decisions belong with qualified professionals and official drug labeling.
